Text 2 Speech help centre

This is the reference for Text 2 Speech, the free online text-to-speech converter at text-2-speech.com. It lists every feature, the exact limits, the file formats you can download, how long files are kept, what the tool does not do, and how to fix the problems people run into most often.

Features

Text 2 Speech converts typed, pasted or imported text into speech with a chosen neural voice and returns an audio file plus two subtitle files.

FeatureHow it works
Voices300+ Microsoft neural voices in 75 languages, female and male. Filter by gender, or search by voice name, country or language (for example India). See the full voice list.
Voice samplesThe Preview button next to a voice plays a short sample sentence in that voice.
Quick read-backPreview Audio reads the first 1,200 characters of your text instantly with a voice built into your own browser or device, not with the selected neural voice.
Speed and pitchTick Speed or Pitch to unlock a slider (steps of 5) and a number box for exact values.
PausesType [break:500ms] or [break:1.5s] anywhere in the text. Ctrl+B (⌘+B on a Mac) inserts [break:500ms] at the cursor.
File importImport File loads a .txt, .md or .text file into the text box as-is, after asking before it replaces existing text.
OutputMP3 or WAV, chosen before you generate, plus SRT and VTT subtitles with every generation.
Results listEach result has a player, download links and a Delete button. Autoplay after generation plays new results automatically.
Interface languages25, for example /es/, /de/ and /zh-CN/. The guides and this help centre are in English.
PriceFree, with no sign-up.

What Text 2 Speech does not do

Text 2 Speech is a browser-based converter for plain text; these features are not available:

  • No accounts. The sign-in page is a placeholder; there is no saved history or favourites, and everything works signed out.
  • No developer API. There are no API keys and no API documentation; the converter is used through the web page.
  • No PDF, Word, e-book or web-page reading. Only plain-text files can be imported; copy the text out of other documents first.
  • No offline mode. The speech is made by an online service, so the converter needs an internet connection, and there is no app to install.
  • No voice cloning or custom voices. Only the voices in the list can be used.
  • No SSML, speaking styles or emotions. SSML tags are not interpreted; speed and pitch are the only voice controls, and pauses come from punctuation and [break:…] markers.
  • No long-term storage. Download anything you want to keep.
  • No commercial-use guarantee. Check the terms of service and the rights in your source text.

Troubleshooting

Most problems come from a language mismatch between the text and the voice, from the rate limit, or from files that have already expired.

No sound when I press Preview Audio

Preview Audio uses the speech voices installed in your browser or operating system, not the selected neural voice. If your device has no voice for that language, the preview can be silent or sound unlike your choice; some browsers have no speech support at all and show Speech preview is not supported in this browser. Check that the tab and device are not muted, then use the Preview button next to a voice, or Generate Audio, to hear the real voice.

The voice sounds wrong, or no audio came back for the text

Set Voice Language to the language your text is written in. A voice is built for its own language: with text in another language it may still produce audio, or it returns nothing when it cannot read the script: Hindi text sent to a US English voice fails with The speech service returned no audio for this text. For text that mixes languages, try a voice marked (Multilingual); see how to choose a voice.

Long text is slow, or generation fails

In testing, a full 5,000-character English text finished in about 12 seconds, so a wait of minutes points to a problem. Long text is processed in pieces of up to 1,500 characters; a piece that fails is retried once, and if it fails again you see Synthesis failed, please retry. Try again, or split the text into shorter parts. Over 5,000 characters, Generate Audio stays disabled until you shorten the text.

"This voice is currently unavailable from the speech service"

The speech service occasionally stops answering for a particular voice. Choose another voice for now. The server tests every voice every six hours and hides a voice from the list after eight failed attempts in a row, until it works again.

"Too many requests — please wait a minute and try again"

The site allows about 20 requests per minute from one IP address, and each voice Preview click is a request. Wait a minute and generate again; your text and settings stay in the page.

A result or a download link disappeared

Results belong to the browser tab they were made in, and the files are deleted about two hours after your last generation. A new tab starts with an empty list, and a reload removes results whose files have expired. Generate the text again and download it straight away.

A pause marker is ignored or rejected

Markers must look like [break:500ms] or [break:2s]; decimals such as [break:1.5s] work, and anything else is read as text. The limits are 25 markers, 30 seconds per marker and 60 seconds in total.

The voice list does not load

The page shows Could not load the voice catalog. Check your connection and reload. when the voice list cannot be fetched. Check your connection and reload the page; if it keeps happening, the speech service may be briefly unreachable.

Subtitles that drift from the audio are covered in the subtitles guide.
