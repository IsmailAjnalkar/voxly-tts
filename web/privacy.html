Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Text 2 Speech is designed to need as little of your data as possible. You can use the converter without an account and without giving us your name or email.

Text you convert

Text you submit is sent to Microsoft's online speech service, which turns it into audio. The audio and subtitle files are stored on our server under a random session identifier and deleted automatically about two hours after your last generation. They are never published or indexed.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Your theme, interface language and the results list of the current tab are kept only in your own browser's storage.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not keep your generated audio after it expires.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reach us through the Support page. Messages sent there (your email address and text) are kept only as long as needed to answer you.
