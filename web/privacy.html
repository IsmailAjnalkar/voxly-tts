Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Voxly is designed to need as little of your data as possible. You can use the converter without an account and without giving us your name or email.

Text you convert

Text you submit is sent to the speech engine only to produce your audio. Generated files are stored against a random session identifier and deleted automatically after a short period; they are never published, indexed or used to train models.

Cookies and analytics

If you accept the cookie banner, we use privacy-respecting analytics cookies to count visits and see which features are popular. Declining changes nothing about how the tool works.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not keep your generated audio after your session ends.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reach us through the Support page.
