How to choose a text to speech voice and accent

Short answer: set Voice Language to the language of your text first, then choose among its voices: the country code after each name (US, GB, IN, AU…) is the accent. Compare two or three with their Preview buttons. For text that mixes languages, try a voice marked (Multilingual).

Match the language of your text

A voice reads best in its own language, so the Voice Language list decides which voices you see. When text and voice do not match, the voice may still produce audio (a US English voice read a German sentence in a test) but it is not a voice for that language, or it returns no audio at all when it cannot read the script, as happened with Hindi text and an English voice. All 75 languages and their voices are on the voices and languages page.

Find an accent

An accent in Text 2 Speech is a regional variant of a language, shown as the country code after the voice name, such as Sonia - GB for British English. All regional variants of a language appear together under that language. To narrow the list, type a country into Voice Search: United Kingdom or India works, as does a voice name. Other languages have regional variants too: Spanish has 22 (for example Spain, Mexico and the US), Arabic 16, French 4 (France, Canada, Belgium, Switzerland) and Portuguese 2 (Brazil and Portugal). Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu voices are listed under their own languages.

Multilingual voices

Multilingual voices are voices that Microsoft names Multilingual: they are intended to read more than one language with the same voice, which suits text that mixes languages. Twelve were in the catalog on 31 October 2026, each listed under its home language:

  • English: Andrew, Ava, Brian and Emma (US), William (AU)
  • German: Florian, Seraphina
  • French: Remy, Vivienne
  • Italian: Giuseppe · Korean: Hyunsu · Portuguese: Thalita (Brazil)

Type multilingual into Voice Search to list the ones in the selected language. Test one with a paragraph of your real text before relying on it.

Compare voices quickly

  1. Use the Gender filter to show only female or male voices. Most regional variants (126 of 142 on 31 October 2026) have exactly one female and one male voice.
  2. Press Preview next to each candidate to hear a short sample sentence in that voice.
  3. Generate the same first paragraph of your text with your two or three favourites. Each result is labelled with its voice name, so you can play them one after another.
  4. Keep the winner, and use the same voice, speed and pitch for every part of the project.

The Preview Audio button under the text box is not a voice sample: it uses your device's own voice. See the help centre for details.

Limitations

  • You cannot create, clone or upload a voice; only catalog voices are available.
  • A voice can be missing from the list for a while if the speech service stops answering for it.
  • There are no speaking styles or emotion settings; speed and pitch are the only adjustments.
