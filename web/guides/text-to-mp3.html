How to convert text to MP3 for free

Short answer: paste your text into Text 2 Speech, choose a voice, leave the output format on MP3 and press Generate Audio, then click ⬇ MP3 in the result. It is free with no sign-up, each conversion takes up to 5,000 characters, and you get a 48 kbps mono MP3 plus subtitles.

Steps

  1. Open the converter. The Voice Language list starts on English.
  2. Set Voice Language to the language of your text, then select a voice. Its Preview button plays a sample.
  3. Paste your text into Input Text, or press Import File to load a .txt, .md or .text file. The counter shows how many of the 5,000 characters you have used.
  4. Optional: tick Speed or Pitch to change them, and add pauses with [break:1s] or Ctrl+B.
  5. Check that Output Format shows MP3 selected; it is the default.
  6. Press Generate Audio. The result appears under Results with a player.
  7. Click ⬇ MP3 to save the file. ⬇ SRT and ⬇ VTT save the matching subtitles.

What the MP3 file contains

The download is a standard MP3 that plays on practically any phone, computer or browser.

PropertyValue
EncodingMP3 at 48 kbps, 24 kHz sample rate, mono
SizeAbout 0.36 MB per minute of speech
Length5,000 characters of English gave 5 min 28 s of speech (voice Aria, US, tested 31 October 2026)
Time to convertAbout 12 seconds for those 5,000 characters in the same test
File nameRandom, for example ddfd8759b889.mp3; rename it after downloading
Kept onlineAbout two hours after your last generation

WAV is also available, but it is about eight times larger and sounds the same; MP3 vs WAV explains when it is worth it.

Text longer than 5,000 characters

To turn a longer document, such as a book chapter, into MP3, convert it in parts and join them afterwards:

  1. Split the text at paragraph or section breaks into parts of up to 5,000 characters.
  2. Convert each part with the same voice, speed and pitch, so the parts sound alike.
  3. Download and rename each file in order (part-01.mp3, part-02.mp3…) before the two-hour expiry.
  4. Join the files in an audio editor, or simply play them as a playlist.

Limitations

  • One voice per file: for a dialogue, convert each speaker's lines separately.
  • The input is plain text; the tool cannot open PDF, Word or web pages.
  • Files are not stored long-term and there is no account history.
