Text to speech voices and languages

Choose the language of your text in the converter, then try two or three voices: voices in the same language can sound very different.

The list comes straight from the speech service and is refreshed daily. Voices that stop producing audio are hidden until they work again. Multilingual voices can read mixed-language text.
