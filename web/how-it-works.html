How Text 2 Speech works

Text 2 Speech (text-2-speech.com) is a free online text-to-speech tool. You paste or type text, pick one of 300+ neural voices in 75 languages, and download the result as an MP3 or WAV file together with SRT and VTT subtitles. No account is needed.

Is Text 2 Speech free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free, and no. Generating, listening and downloading need no sign-up and no payment details. There is no paid tier.

Which voices does it use?

The voices are Microsoft's online neural text-to-speech voices. The live list currently has 300+ voices across 75 languages and regional variants, female and male, plus a few multilingual voices that handle mixed-language text. The voices and languages page lists them all. The service checks every voice every six hours and temporarily hides any voice that stops producing audio, so the list only shows voices that work.

What happens when I press Generate Audio?

  1. Your text is split at any [break:…] pause markers, and long passages are split at sentence ends into chunks of at most 1,500 characters.
  2. Each chunk is sent to the speech service with the voice, speed and pitch you chose; up to four chunks are synthesized at the same time, and a chunk that fails is retried once.
  3. The audio pieces and any pauses are joined in order into one MP3 file (48 kbps, 24 kHz, mono). If you chose WAV, that file is converted to 16-bit PCM WAV (24 kHz, mono).
  4. The speech engine reports when each word starts and ends. Those timings become the SRT and VTT subtitle files, with your original punctuation kept.

Limits

WhatLimit
Text per generation5,000 characters (an emoji counts as one character)
Pause markersUp to 25 per generation, at most 30 seconds each and 60 seconds in total
Speed−100% to +100%
Pitch−100 Hz to +100 Hz
RequestsAbout 20 generations per minute from one IP address; voice previews count too
FormatsMP3 or WAV audio; SRT and VTT subtitles with every generation

For longer material, such as book chapters, generate it in parts and join the files in an audio editor.

What happens to my text and audio?

Your text is sent to Microsoft's online speech service to be turned into audio. The finished audio and subtitle files are stored on our server under a random session identifier and are deleted automatically about two hours after your last generation. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Details are in the privacy policy.

What Text 2 Speech does not do

  • No voice cloning or custom voices: only the listed neural voices are available.
  • No accounts, saved history or favourites yet. Results stay in your browser tab for the current session.
  • No guarantee on commercial use: check the terms and the rights in your source text.
